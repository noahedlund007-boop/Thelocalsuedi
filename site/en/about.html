Noah Edlund

Photographer and filmmaker from Hälsingland, Sweden.

I photograph and film people, places and situations that haven’t been arranged. What happens in front of the camera interests me more than what I can set up, and a picture should say something, not just be technically right.

Hälsingland is where I’m from and a big part of what I do. I shoot both digital and analogue, and film and medium format are a big interest. I like dark rooms, available light and places with character.

I want to do more documentary work: longer stories about people and society, music, and my own projects. Alongside that I take assignments: portraits, events, concerts and film.

Available for assignments.
